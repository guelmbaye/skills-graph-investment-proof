WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM

Boundaries of the result

All training programs and resource capacities in this demo are fictional. Allocation follows a fixed priority, not globally optimal scheduling. No claim is made about actual competencies acquired, employment outcomes, or superiority over SQL, JSON joins or OR-Tools.
